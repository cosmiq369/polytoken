# PolyToken submission

## Sponsor technology explanation

PolyToken investigates why Indian-language AI workloads cost more than equivalent English content, identifies the responsible chat components, and tests a proposed change. Its central output is an explanation with inspectable evidence, including failed experiments and reasons to withhold a recommendation. The demo companies and conversations are fictional; native review remains pending.

**Zetaris** is the investigation's data layer. The agent reads company configuration, chats, token measurements, reference sentences and verified prices through SQL. A federation query joins company and measurement views with reference coverage and the REST price source. Direct PostgreSQL access is limited to setup and measurement writes. The installed connector's schema restriction is handled with explicit public views, and the REST table is refreshed when its previously empty schema lacks price columns. Both workarounds are recorded. See [registration](../scripts/register_zetaris_sources.py), [federation SQL](../sql/token_ratio_by_part.sql), and [query evidence](../logs/sample/p04_federation.jsonl).

**Meterless** supplies the reference architectures, independently implemented in Python. World Model stores sourced, timestamped observations and their supporting records. H-MEM retains company profiles and earlier findings with a trust ledger. Markovian runs bounded reasoning chunks and records projected versus actual token use, including signed savings when compression costs extra tokens. The implementation ports all 29 pinned conformance checks. It does not claim to run a Meterless hosted service. See [specification attribution and checks](METERLESS.md), [implementations](../src/polytoken/meterless/), and [signed accounting](../logs/sample/meterless_signed_audit.jsonl).

**NVIDIA** provides the model API. Nemotron plans and judges through one injected generator; its deployment name, endpoint and rate limit come from the environment. A shared limiter, physical-attempt budgets, bounded retries and separate reasoning and background deadlines constrain work. Hosted models generate independent control and changed replies and perform timing trials. Eligibility requires recorded serving probes, not just catalogue presence. See [generator](../src/polytoken/llm.py), [probe results](../logs/sample/hosted_probe.jsonl), and [contextual quality protocol](../src/polytoken/agent/quality.py).

The deterministic checker authenticates evidence and recomputes numeric claims before reporting. Both demo investigations reach their planted top finding. Neither tested change is recommended: the unchanged A/A controls fail the same quality threshold used for A/B, making the result inconclusive. The UI exposes this limitation alongside cost scenarios and source receipts. Cached observations retain their original provenance; the new demo is a fresh investigation, not an independent quality replication. See [contract score](../logs/sample/contract_score.md).

**Cursor was not used.** Codex and Claude assisted development. The pre-existing measurement harness, corpus and experiment results are disclosed in [DECLARATIONS.md](DECLARATIONS.md); independent reviewer checks are preserved separately.

## Project summary

PolyToken explains why an Indian-language AI workload costs more than equivalent English content, then tests a change without hiding uncertainty. A product lead selects a fictional Telugu support bot or Hindi voice assistant. The agent queries chats, measurements, references and prices through Zetaris, traces costs through repeated instructions and conversation history, and runs a bounded experiment on NVIDIA hosted models. Nemotron plans and judges; independently implemented Meterless architectures preserve evidence, company memory and compressed reasoning state. A deterministic checker recomputes every numeric claim before the Token Ledger displays it with a source receipt. Samudra's English system prompt reduces reconstructed cost, but its quality result is inconclusive because even unchanged control replies fail the judge's equivalence bar. Swara's answer cap also remains unapproved. Native review is pending. PolyToken reports those limits openly, distinguishing measured observations, quoted billing scenarios and hypothetical price changes so teams can decide what evidence to collect next.
